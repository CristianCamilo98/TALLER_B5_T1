All figures are generated by [`reports/build_final_figures.py`](../build_final_figures.py)
from [`reports/final_analysis/*.csv`](.) (and, only for the log_return
distribution figure, `artifacts/final/strict_final_20260902/fidelity/tables/marginal_statistics.csv`).
Model names only — `Bootstrap + Jitter`, `WGAN-GP`, `DDPM`, `TimeVAE`,
`Normalizing Flow` — are used as labels; internal `method_id` values
(`bootstrap_jitter`, `cristian`, `daniel`, `marco`, `david`) exist only in

### How to read each figure / what NOT to conclude

- **01 / 01b — RMSE by synthetic share.** Read: lower line = better. Error bars are ±1 SD over 3 seeds, not a confidence interval. Do not conclude a method is "significantly" better from bar/line overlap alone — see `seed_stability.csv` for the underlying spread.
- **02 — Improvement heatmap.** All values are `real_only_rmse − method_rmse`, expressed as a percentage of `real_only_rmse`; positive = improvement, by construction (verified in `sanity_check_improvements.csv`). Do not compare cell colors across figures — the color scale is local to this heatmap.
- **03 / 03b — Performance at 75%.** Ranking is computed from `master_utility_table.csv` at ratio=0.75, not hardcoded. Do not extrapolate this ranking to 25%/50% — see figures 01/02 for that.
- **04 — Seed stability.** n=3 seeds; ranges are min–max, not a statistical confidence interval. Do not read "most stable" as "best" — TimeVAE is the counterexample.
- **05 — Neural vs simple baseline.** Zero line means "no different from Bootstrap + Jitter." Do not read the magnitude as effect size on the original RMSE scale — it is a difference of two already-relative improvement percentages (percentage points).
- **06 — C2ST fidelity.** 0.50 is the "indistinguishable" reference; do not read this chart as a single quality score, and do not treat Bootstrap + Jitter's strong score as evidence of "good generation" — it is a structural property of bootstrapping real data.
- **Fidelity metric summary.** Each panel has its own y-axis; do not compare absolute bar heights across panels, and do not sum/average panels into a ranking.
- **07 — Fidelity vs utility.** n=5 points, no fitted line, Pearson/Spearman values live in `fidelity_utility_correlations.csv`. Do not infer causality or statistical significance from this figure.
- **log_return distribution.** Central 99% view (p01–p99); outlier tails beyond that are intentionally not drawn to keep the chart legible, but every underlying calculation in this project uses the complete data, not this clipped view.

### Main numbers referenced above

- Real-Only reference: RMSE ≈ 1.479584, MAE ≈ 1.146934.
- Best RMSE overall: DDPM @ 75%, RMSE ≈ 0.2446 (+83.5%).
- Best MAE overall: Normalizing Flow @ 75%, MAE ≈ 0.1740 (+84.8%).
- Most seed-stable: TimeVAE @ 25% (RMSE std ≈ 0.0006). Least seed-stable: DDPM @ 50% (RMSE std ≈ 0.0638).

## Presentation shortlist (5-minute talk)

**MUST_SHOW**

1. **`02_rmse_improvement_heatmap`** — the headline number (74–84% improvement) in one glance, per method and ratio.
2. **`03_performance_at_75pct`** — who actually wins, with both RMSE and the improvement % in one bar.
3. **`05_neural_vs_simple_baseline`** — directly answers "do the neural generators actually add anything over a simple method?", every bar labeled.

**REVIEW_FOR_PRESENTATION** (include if the talk has room for a 4th–5th slide)

- **`07_fidelity_vs_utility`** — closes the loop between the fidelity work and the utility result. Kept out of MUST_SHOW only because it needs the most caveats (n=5, exploratory, no trend line) to present correctly in the time available for one slide — the underlying story (return-channel fidelity metrics track utility fairly strongly; nearest-neighbour and volume-channel Wasserstein do not) is genuinely one of the stronger findings in `ANALYSIS.md` Section 7, so include it if there is time to state the caveat out loud.
- **`01b_rmse_by_synthetic_share_zoom`** — useful if the audience needs to see the per-ratio curves and DDPM's 50% variance directly, rather than only the heatmap summary.

**BACKUP / GITHUB** (repo evidence and generator READMEs, not the live talk)

- `06_c2st_fidelity` — approved as-is; a fidelity detail better suited to a generator-level README or appendix slide.
- `04_seed_stability` — the full stability picture; reference if a stability question comes up in Q&A.
- `01_rmse_by_synthetic_share` (full-range) — kept as GitHub/evidence: Real-Only ≈ 1.48 compresses the five method curves too much for a presentation lead; `01b` is the presentation-appropriate version.
